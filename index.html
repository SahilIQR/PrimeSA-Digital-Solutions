What happens when I request a website?

We’ll start by talking about your business and what you need. Before work begins, we’ll agree on a project scope and confirm the price.

How long will a website take?

Timing depends on the agreed scope and the information and materials available. We can discuss a realistic timeframe before the project starts.

Do I need a domain name or hosting?

It depends on your project. We can discuss what you already have and what may be needed. Any domain or hosting arrangements and costs must be agreed separately; none are assumed in the introductory prices.

What about payments?

Payment arrangements will be discussed and agreed with the project scope before work begins. The website does not accept payments.

Can you help maintain a website?

Website maintenance is offered as a starting point for discussion. The tasks and monthly scope need to be agreed before maintenance work begins.

How do I ask for a quotation?

Send a message on WhatsApp with a short description of your business and what you have in mind. We’ll use that to start a conversation about scope and pricing.
